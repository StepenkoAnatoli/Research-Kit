# Discovery Contract - when does the EUDR actually apply?

Started 2026-09-22.

## Build intent

A compliance question with a date answer, chosen deliberately from a domain this project
knows nothing about, to test whether the kit produces research a builder could act on
rather than research that merely passes its own checks.

"Done" is the operative application dates as of today, from the authority that sets them,
with the amending instrument named.

**A prior was registered before any collection, so the corpus could contradict it.** It did.
The prior said 30 December 2025 for large operators and 30 June 2026 for micro and small
enterprises, at high confidence, with an explicit note that a second postponement decided
after May 2026 could not be known. There was a second postponement.

## Unknowns

| ID | Unknown | Why it blocks the build | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| U-1 | What are the operative EUDR application dates today? | Any compliance plan is scheduled off this date. A year wrong in either direction is either wasted spend or a missed obligation | CLOSED | E-01: the Commission states it directly - large and medium operators **30 December 2026**, micro and small operators **30 June 2027**, and micro and small operators already covered by the EU Timber Regulation **30 December 2026** [single-witness: the European Commission is the authority that sets and publishes these dates, so a second party can only report them. One of the secondary rows in this corpus demonstrates what that reporting is worth - its own prose still carries the superseded pair while its structured data is current] |
| U-2 | How many times has application been postponed, and by what instrument? | A date that has already moved twice is a date that may move again. Knowing the mechanism is how a plan stays correct rather than lucky | CLOSED | E-06 records the sequence: 30 December 2024 struck through, then 30 December 2025 struck through - **two postponements**, not one. E-08 names the outcome of the second in its title: "EU Deforestation Regulation application postponed to 30 December 2026" |
| U-3 | Does the smallest-operator deferral apply to everyone small? | A carve-out that catches an operator a year early is exactly the kind of detail a summary drops | CLOSED | E-01: **no.** Micro and small operators get 30 June 2027 **unless already covered by the EU Timber Regulation**, in which case the earlier date applies. Neither the registered prior nor a general web search surfaced this; only the Commission page states it [single-witness: the EUTR carve-out is stated only by the Commission page. A general web search on the same question did not surface it at all, which is the point - a summary drops the exception and keeps the headline date] |

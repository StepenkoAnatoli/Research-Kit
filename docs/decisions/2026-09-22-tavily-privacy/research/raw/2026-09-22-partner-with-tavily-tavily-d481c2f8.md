## Find the right track

Whether you're building agents, advising enterprises, or running a marketplace, there's a path to partner with Tavily.

Enterprise Distribution

### Marketplace

Our marketplace partners distribute Tavily's API through their cloud platforms and tool catalogs, giving developers one-click access to real-time web search for AI agents.

Available on

#### Co-Branded Launch Campaigns

Joint press releases, blog posts, and social amplification when your listing goes live and at key milestones.

#### Dedicated Partner Manager

A single point of contact to coordinate listing updates, align on go-to-market, and drive adoption together.

#### Joint Customer Case Studies

Co-authored case studies highlighting shared customers who are using Tavily through your platform.

#### Priority Support for Shared Customers

Escalation paths and priority technical support for mutual customers using Tavily through your marketplace.

#### Co-Selling with Tavily

Joint sales motions where Tavily's team sells alongside yours to land and expand shared accounts.

Services & Implementation

### SI / Consultancy

Our consulting partners work with enterprise customers to design, implement, and scale production agent systems powered by Tavily's web access layer.

#### Tavily Certification & Partner Badge

Official certification on Tavily's API surface. Display your partner badge to differentiate your practice.

#### Joint Selling

Collaborate with Tavily's sales team to close enterprise deals together.

#### Sales Enablement Materials

Pitch decks, demo environments, and solution briefs to help your team position Tavily in client conversations.

#### Dedicated Partner Support

Direct access to Tavily's team for technical escalations, deal support, and coordination.

Integration & Reach

### Tech Partner

Our tech partners integrate Tavily as a native, built-in tool within their developer frameworks and agent orchestration platforms.

#### Co-Maintained Integration & Docs

We build and maintain the integration alongside your team, including SDK support, reference docs, and code samples.

#### Developer Community Promotion

Featured to Tavily's 1M+ developer community through our blog, changelogs, docs, and social channels.

#### Early Access to New Endpoints

Preview upcoming API changes and new endpoints before public release so your integration stays ahead.

#### Joint Developer Content

Co-authored tutorials, quickstart guides, and video walkthroughs to drive adoption within your developer community.

VCs, Accelerators & Startup Programs

### Accelerators

We work with VC firms and accelerators to extend startup-friendly pricing and benefits to their portfolio companies, and we offer a startup program for early-stage companies building with Tavily.

#### Startup-Friendly Pricing

Discounted access to Tavily's API so you can build and iterate without worrying about cost while you find product-market fit.

#### Self-Serve Developer Resources

Full access to Tavily's developer docs, implementation guides, and code samples to get up and running on your own.

#### Milestone Co-Marketing

Spotlight features on our blog and social channels as you ship new use cases and hit growth milestones with Tavily.

#### Co-Branded Case Study

As you scale, we partner on a joint case study to showcase what you've built and drive visibility for both teams.

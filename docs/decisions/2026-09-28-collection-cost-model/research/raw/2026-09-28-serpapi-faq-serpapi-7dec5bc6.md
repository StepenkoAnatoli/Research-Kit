# FAQ

Got a question? We're here to answer!

#### Can I request that SerpApi add an API for scraping another search engine?

Of course! We are always expanding our code and building new APIs for different search engines. You can create a feature request on our [Public Roadmap](https://github.com/serpapi/public-roadmap).

We aren't able to build APIs for every search engine for a variety of reasons but we do always make additions where they are needed and able to be done.

#### Do my unused searches rollover to the next month?

Your monthly subscription restarts with your monthly allowance of successful search credits on the first day of your billing cycle's subscription.

#### Do you offer technical support for users?

Yes! We are always happy to help users to be able to use our API! You can email us at [contact@serpapi.com](mailto:contact@serpapi.com) or leave us a live chat message and we'll try and get back to you within a single business day.

#### Do you provide SLA guarantees?

We provide a 99.95% SLA guarantee with all plans. The SLA guarantee comes with a 100% penalty, and we offer credits up to 100% of the monthly price of your plan on your next invoice.

#### How are searches counted?

Only successful searches are counted towards your monthly searches. Cached, errored, and failed searches are not. The number of results returned per response will not affect the number of credits used. Responses with 100 results or empty result sets will both cost 1 search credit.

#### How long does SerpApi keep the JSON, Markdown, and raw HTML files for searches?

We store all the JSON, Markdown, and HTML files for your searches for 31 days after the search is made. Your record of searches can be found on our [dashboard](https://serpapi.com/searches).

#### I encountered a bug, where do I report it to get it fixed?

If you find a bug or our scraper is missing part of the page, you can create a bug post on our [Public Roadmap](https://github.com/serpapi/public-roadmap). Be sure to include the search ID for the problematic search and any helpful screenshots or description of the problem.

#### What are scraper legal protections?

Our U.S. Legal Shield covers the scraping and parsing of search engine data, as long as your use of the data or service is not illegal. Such illegal use includes, by way of example and not limitation, copyright infringement, DMCA violations, other intellectual property infringement, privacy violations, fraud, harassment, cybercrime, terrorism, child exploitation, denial-of-service attacks, or war crimes. In short, we assume liability for the lawful collection of public search data (scraping, parsing, and related actions), but not for how that data is ultimately used. U.S. Legal Shield is included with the Production Plan and above, with up to $2 million in coverage. [Learn more.](https://serpapi.com/us-legal-shield)

#### What is SerpApi's compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and SOC 2?

SerpApi, LLC is SOC 2 and SOC 3 certified. A copy of our report is available on request at [security@serpapi.com](mailto:security@serpapi.com).

We don't give access, sell, or share your data with any third party for any reason. The only exceptions are services needed to process your search, search debugging, and internal usage tools. Your data is defined by your account data, your search metadata and parameters, your search contents, and any other data you share with SerpApi, LLC.

Please note that under the "Right to be forgotten" provision of the GDPR regulation, accessing searches from U.S. engines through our service might show results that are not included in the EU results as these are protected under the First Amendment of the U.S. constitution.

[Learn more](https://serpapi.com/security)

#### What's your guaranteed throughput?

The hourly throughput limit for plans with under 1 million searches per month is 20% of your plan volume. For example, the Developer Plan includes 5,000 searches per month, so subscribers to the Developer Plan can use up to 1,000 successful searches per hour. For plans with 1 million or more searches per month, the hourly throughput is 100,000 + 1% of your plan volume. While we have no other specific rate limit, we recommend spreading your searches evenly throughout each hour for the best performance.

#### Why does the results page I got with SerpApi look different than what I get when I manually do the search myself?

There can be many reasons for this. Usually, it's because the searches were done with different parameters. Location, language, and other parameters can affect what results are served even if the keyword for the search is the same. Search results can also be affected if you are logged into an account with that search engine.

For a more accurate comparison, open the `url` for an equivalent search provided in our JSON response ( for example, in the `search_metadata.google_url` key), in an incognito browser window. If you still see discrepancies between the two after this, feel free to let us know at [contact@serpapi.com](mailto:contact@serpapi.com) and we'll take a look at it.

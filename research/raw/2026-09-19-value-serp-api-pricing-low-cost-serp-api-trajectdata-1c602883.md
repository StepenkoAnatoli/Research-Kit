How are Searches counted?

A Search is any successful call to our Search API. Only successful Searches are counted. There is no charge for calls to our other API’s.

Do you offer a Free Trial?

Absolutely! Our [free trial](https://app.valueserp.com/signup) gives you 100 searches to use to evaluate VALUE SERP. No credit card is required. When you’re ready you may wish to consider one our paid plans.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards – Visa, Mastercard & American Express. All payments are securely handled by our payments partner [Stripe](https://stripe.com/).

How quickly do I get results?

Immediately! VALUE SERP is a real-time API. No queues. No waiting. You can also use Batches to enqueue up to 15,000 searches and run them on a schedule.

Can I change or cancel my plan later?

You certainly can! Upgrade, downgrade or cancel your plan at any time. No strings attached!

Yes, we offer high-volume Enterprise plans. Please [get in touch](https://trajectdata.com/enterprise/) to discuss your requirements.
